SIG-SAIA

Special Interest Group in Spatial Acoustics and Immersive Audio

The Special Interest Group in Spatial Acoustics and Immersive Audio (SIG-SAIA) brings together researchers, practitioners and end-users from academia and industry who work on the spatial side of audio and acoustics.

SIG-SAIA started as a special interest group within the UK Acoustics Network (UKAN) and now runs as an independent group.

Join the mailing list

The SIG-SAIA mailing list is a Google Group. Members receive announcements about meet-ups, seminars, workshops, jobs and calls for papers, and can post to the list.

You can join in either of the following ways:

  • With a Google account: sign in, visit groups.google.com/g/sig-saia/about and click Join group.
  • Without a Google account: send a blank email to sig-saia+subscribe@googlegroups.com from the address you wish to subscribe, then reply to the confirmation email.

If you have already joined the Google Group, you don’t need to join again.

To leave, send a blank email to sig-saia+unsubscribe@googlegroups.com or use the link at the bottom of any list email.

What we cover

  • Acquisition: microphone arrays, beamforming, direction-of-arrival estimation and 6DoF sound field capture.
  • Rendering: loudspeaker arrays, headphone and binaural rendering, and acoustic augmented reality.
  • Perception: spatial hearing, listening experiments and binaural auditory models.
  • Applications: immersive audio production, AR/VR for audiology research and clinical use, auditory training and auditory displays.

What we do

We run topic-focused meet-ups for members, a yearly workshop or showcase, and a seminar series. We also organise joint events with groups such as Immerse UK, the AES, the IEEE and the Institute of Acoustics, along with training events aimed at early-career researchers.

Postdoctoral Research Associate – Spatial Hearing and Machine Learning

Queen Mary University of London. Closing date: 8 December 2026

A 36-month position working with Dr Aidan Hogg on the EPSRC-funded project Spatial Hearing Augmentation to Improve Hearing Assistive Technology, which investigates whether enhanced artificial spatial hearing cues can improve sound localisation, speech understanding in noise and situational awareness for people with hearing loss.

Details
  • Department: School of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science, Centre for Multimodal AI
  • Contract: Full-time, fixed-term (36 months)
  • Salary: £39,187–£47,550 per annum
  • Reference: 11997

The successful candidate will develop personalised head-related transfer function (HRTF) models, apply machine learning and auditory-model optimisation techniques, and design perceptual experiments to evaluate spatial hearing augmentation, in collaboration with Imperial College London and Eargym Ltd.

We are looking for candidates with a PhD (or close to completion), or equivalent research experience, in acoustics, audio signal processing, machine learning, hearing science or a related field. Experience with spatial audio, HRTFs, Python/MATLAB and machine learning is particularly relevant.

Apply on the Queen Mary jobs site. For informal enquiries, email a.hogg@qmul.ac.uk.
